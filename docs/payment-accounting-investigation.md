## Proposed decision, no implementation

If `amount` represents money the customer paid, use effective **gross customer settlement** to extinguish receivables and retain **net cash = gross − processor fee** for cash reporting, with the fee treated according to the approved business expense policy. Example invoice 100, customer payment 100, fee 3: settlement 100, AR 0, cash 97, fee 3. This requires product/accounting approval; this investigation does not establish tax treatment, refund policy or ledger postings.

Change the canonical view, all manual create/update/restore and import/replacement capacity guards, invoice paid floors, PDF fallbacks and reporting labels together under one contract. Preserve workspace, currency, effective status, archive and locking rules. Do not change only the view or label remaining fees as customer debt. If net AR remains an explicitly approved restricted policy, predictions and PDF fallback still need agreement with net; do not certify payment editing until generated/trigger behavior and the guard are resolved.

Required isolated regressions: gross100/fee3/net97; zero fee; null net; generated net and ordinary net with/without trigger; fee/amount edits exceeding capacity; new/repeated/reassigned imports; partial payments across months; pending/completed/paid/null/empty/refunded/reversed status boundaries; archive/restore; invoice amount reduction and re-import; rounding/tolerance; currency isolation; active/public/archived PDF parity and later-page failure.
